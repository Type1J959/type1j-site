SATISFACTORY 1.0 FACTORY & POWER GUIDE

Frequently Asked Questions — Satisfactory 1.0 Factory Flow Planner

▶ Satisfactory Stream Archive 🟣 Live on Twitch
Does this Satisfactory 1.0 calculator handle Refinery split outputs and byproducts like Heavy Oil Residue? ▾

Yes. All multi-output Refinery, Blender, Quantum Encoder, and Nuclear recipes display every primary and secondary output (for example, Rubber converts 30 m³/min Crude Oil into 20/min Rubber plus 20 m³/min Heavy Oil Residue). Byproducts automatically feed downstream machines like Residual Fuel (60 m³/min Heavy Oil Residue to 40 m³/min Fuel) with zero false raw material shortages, and you can optionally route byproducts to Fluid Buffers.

How do Coal, Fuel, and Nuclear Power Generators work in the Satisfactory 1.0 planner? ▾

You can place numbered Biomass Burners (30 MW), Coal-Powered Generators (75 MW using 15/min Coal + 45 m³/min Water), Fuel-Powered Generators (250 MW using 20 m³/min Fuel, 7.5 m³/min Turbofuel, or 4.17 m³/min Rocket Fuel), and Nuclear Power Plants (2,500 MW + 240 m³/min Water). Generator fuel and water are deducted directly from your Material Balance and scale linearly with clock speed per Satisfactory 1.0 rules, showing live Net Grid MW.

How does the Can I Add Expansion Advisor determine if I need a new resource node? ▾

The Expansion Advisor scans every saved factory from Start to Finish using whole 100% machines (preserving Power Shards). At every stage it first taps spare output from your existing machines and smelters, then checks untapped scouted nodes and 100% miner tier upgrades, and only tells you to go scout a new node when your currently identified nodes cannot cover the remaining raw ore or fluid demand.
