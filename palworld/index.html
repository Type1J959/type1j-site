How does Palworld 1.0 breeding power (CombiRank) calculation work? ▾

Every Pal in Palworld has a hidden CombiRank breeding power value. Standard breeding calculates floor((Parent1 + Parent2 + 1) / 2) and selects the Pal whose CombiRank is closest to that average, while special Unique Combinations and Self-Only Legendaries override the standard formula. This hub supports both forward parent lookup and reverse child lookup.

Does this Palworld Hub include 1.0 World Tree content, Raid Bosses, and Schematics? ▾

Yes. The Type 1 J Palworld 1.0 Hub includes both the Palpagos Islands and World Tree interactive maps, all 9 Tower Bosses, 15 Raid and World Bosses with recommended strategies and coordinates, key NPCs/Merchants, and Ancient Ruin / Alpha Schematic drop locations.
