CORE CANON LORE 📍 Old Asylum → Sunny Meadows Mental Institution

Case File #07: Patient 07 ("Lisa")

The single darkest connected storyline in Phasmophobia, told entirely through UV wall scratches, padded cells, and the basement morgue.

  • In the original Asylum map, investigators using UV lights discovered desperate messages from a female patient who spent two years suffering inside the crumbling facility.
  • Her writings revealed her last shred of hope: she was being transferred to a brand-new facility called Sunny Meadows, believing the doctors there would finally heal her.
  • Instead, Sunny Meadows proved far worse. Inside her locked padded isolation cell, a UV light reveals thousands of scratched daily tallies — spanning nearly two decades of solitary confinement and medical horror.
  • Her journey ends in the Basement Morgue on the left wing of Sunny Meadows, where her body in a patient gown still lies on a cold metal slab during every investigation.
PLAYABLE EASTER EGG 📍 Sunny Meadows Chapel (Full & Restricted)

The Flaming Chapel Cross & Apparition

A chilling hidden ritual directly tied to Patient 07 that triggers right in front of your squad.

  • When the Summoning Circle spawns in the Sunny Meadows Chapel, lighting all 5 candles does more than summon the contract's ghost.
  • The giant crucifix mounted high on the Chapel wall violently bursts into flames!
  • At the same instant, an apparition of Patient 07 briefly manifests in the chapel before vanishing as the Cursed Hunt erupts.
MAP SECRET & BADGE 📍 Point Hope Lighthouse

Point Hope: Ferryman of the Drowned

An atmospheric maritime mystery hidden across all 10 levels of the isolated coastal lighthouse.

  • Point Hope tells the story of lighthouse keepers driven mad by isolation, shipwrecks, and restless spirits rising from the fog.
  • Hidden nautical clues, UV markings, pool-table numbers, and the top-floor lighthouse bell combine into a multi-step puzzle.
  • Completing the secret unlocks the exclusive "Ferryman of the Drowned" ID Card & Badge for your investigator profile.
NEW MAP SECRET 📍 Nell's Diner (v0.15.0+)

Nell's Diner & The Locked Safe

A retro late-night roadside diner hiding more than just grease traps and a walk-in freezer.

  • Added in the v0.15.0 update, Nell's Diner packs 14 dense rooms into a Tanglewood-sized roadside stop — including a chilling walk-in cooler.
  • Sharp-eyed investigators can track down hidden code clues around the diner to crack open the locked safe tucked in the back.
  • Environmental details across the counter, kitchen, and staff office hint at an abrupt, panicked abandonment mid-shift.
HORROR HOMAGES & LOBBY SECRETS 📍 Maple Lodge, Bleasdale, Grafton & Warehouse Lobby

Classic Map Easter Eggs & Lobby 666

Kinetic Games hid horror movie nods and playful secrets throughout nearly every location.

  • Maple Lodge Campsite: Walk to the very tip of the wooden pier and peer straight down into the lake — Jason Voorhees' hockey mask (Friday the 13th) is staring back up from beneath the surface.
  • Bleasdale Farmhouse: Eagle-eyed players can spot classic horror references (including a Saw-inspired nod) woven into the reworked estate.
  • Grafton Farmhouse: The reworked rotting farmhouse adds the unnerving Doll Room and Mannequin Room, both infamous for sudden ghost prop teleports.
  • Warehouse Lobby: Sink baskets on the lobby hoop all the way to 666 points to trigger a demonic audio/lighting nod — and peek into the locked side rooms for creepy developer surprises!
